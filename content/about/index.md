Sheerio Online is an unofficial, fan-made archive of Ed Sheeran videos: live performances, surprise appearances, collaborations, interviews and the small moments in between. It is not affiliated with Ed Sheeran, his management, his label or YouTube.

{{< archive-stats >}}

## Who maintains it

The site is run by Bojan, a Sheerio based in Austria, as a hobby project. It started in February 2024 and has been growing a few videos at a time ever since.

## What is included

Every entry is a video where Ed is the reason to watch: his own performances, guest spots at other artists' shows, fans he invites on stage, TV and radio appearances, and interviews. Each entry lists the [songs](/songs/) actually heard in the video, so all performances of a song can be found in one place, and [tags](/tags/) for the people and kinds of moment involved.

## How videos are sourced

Videos come from YouTube: official channels, broadcasters and radio stations, and fan footage filmed at shows. Nothing is rehosted here; every video is embedded from YouTube, so views and credit go to whoever uploaded it.

Before a video is added, it is checked for being genuine footage. Channels that post dramatised or AI generated "celebrity incident" clips are common, and anything that looks fabricated or has no trace outside its own upload is left out.

All videos are checked regularly. When one goes private or is removed, it is taken off the site, and restored if it comes back.

## How dates are verified

The date on each entry is the date of the event itself, the concert, broadcast or interview, not the day it was uploaded to YouTube. Uploads often appear days or years later, so the date is worked out from the video title and description, the tour or broadcast it belongs to, or other coverage of the event. Only when none of that pins it down is the upload date used as the closest known date. This is what keeps the [timeline](/timeline/) in the order things actually happened.
