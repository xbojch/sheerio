Ed makes his Austin City Limits debut with a solo loop pedal set from the Multiply era, broadcast on PBS on 11 October 2014. Don't and Sing made the TV episode, while All of the Stars and You Need Me, I Don't Need You were shared as web exclusive bonus tracks.
