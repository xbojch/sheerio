Ed calls in to iHeartRadio Canada's Shannon Burns on the day Bad Habits comes out. He talks about the idea behind the song and its video, credits laser eye surgery for his new look, and opens up about the two big life events that shaped the album, grief, finally having a band, and signing Maisie Peters to Gingerbread Man Records.
