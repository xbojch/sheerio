After Khalid had to cancel his performance at Gillette Stadium in Foxborough, Ed Sheeran asks John Mayer to open for him. John accepts under one condition: he gets to make and sell some funny t-shirts. He designs one that says "Ed (in FedEx style) - the night John Mayer opened for Ed Sheeran", and they sell out in minutes. Later in the night John joins Ed on stage for Thinking Out Loud, and a fan in the crowd films the whole Mathematics Tour show.
