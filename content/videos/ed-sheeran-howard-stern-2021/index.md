Ed tells Howard Stern about the daily phone calls he gets from Elton John, and admits he did not always follow the advice, including the time Elton pushed him to sign a 15 year old Shawn Mendes to his label. He also explains how his love of ketchup led to his very own Heinz commercial. Ed also opens up about testing positive for COVID just before his album release, how his recovery went, and why he had to cancel his Saturday Night Live appearance.
