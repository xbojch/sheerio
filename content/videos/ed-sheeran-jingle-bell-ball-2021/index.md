Ed closes Capital's Jingle Bell Ball at The O2 with a ten song headline set that runs from Bloodstream and Shivers through Castle On The Hill, Perfect and Bad Habits. Anne-Marie walks out halfway through for 2002, and Merry Christmas gets the whole arena singing. Capital's own camera crew also filmed Castle on the Hill and the 2002 duet in full, added here as extra clips from the same night.
