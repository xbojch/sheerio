A miniseries by The Pulse about the crew behind the Mathematics Tour. At Nissan Stadium in Nashville, RF expert Dave White explains how the wireless setup copes with the in the round stage and production manager Chris Marsh looks back on more than 13 years with Ed, before Dave leads a tour beneath the stage through Guitar World, Looper World and the automation crew.
