Ed joins Aitch on My G, the standout track from Close To Home that Aitch dedicated to his sister Gracie, with Ed singing the chorus that promises to always protect her. Funds from the video went to the Down's Syndrome Association, and it remains one of the warmest collaborations Ed has been part of.
