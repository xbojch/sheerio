Ed Sheeran plays an intimate set at Dublin's Ruby Sessions, weaving Don't into a loop pedal medley of Loyal, No Diggity, The Next Episode and Nina. A stripped back showcase of his looping and beatboxing in a tiny room. He also steps off the stage and plays Give Me Love right in the middle of the crowd.
