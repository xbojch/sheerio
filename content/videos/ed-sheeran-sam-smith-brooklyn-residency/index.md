Ed turns up at Warsaw in Brooklyn during Sam Smith's To Be Free residency, first for their duet "Who We Love" and then alongside Brandi Carlile for "I'm Not The Only One". Two songs from the same night in a room that holds only a few hundred people, one filmed from the crowd and one from Sam's own camera crew.
