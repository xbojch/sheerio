Ed records a session for The Live Room at Hinge Studios in Chicago, just him, his guitar and his loop pedal building everything up in real time. He plays You Need Me, I Don't Need You and Give Me Love, and takes on Nina Simone's Be My Husband, stomping out the rhythm for a raw, bluesy cover.
