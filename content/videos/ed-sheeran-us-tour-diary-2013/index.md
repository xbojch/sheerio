Ed's US Tour Diary, filmed and edited by Gregory Kaplan. In part one Ed shows off his table tennis skills, hangs out with fans for Q100 radio and takes us behind the scenes of his shows. Part two hits Nashville, where he freestyles with Foy Vance and Rizzle Kicks and beats everyone at Monopoly. In part three he passes through Detroit, gets a bowling pin as a gift and sings Man of Constant Sorrow with Rizzle Kicks. Part four reaches New York City, where Ed meets the $2 bill man, headlines Radio City and gets a special backstage performance from Rizzle Kicks. In part five he reaches California, has his show invaded by Stormtroopers and meets Elton John. The final part sees him perform with Rizzle Kicks, meet a life size Lego man and dress as a pirate for a very special show.
