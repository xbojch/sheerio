Ed's tour diaries from the x era, filmed and edited by Murray Cummings. In part one he plays a show on a boat and one at a school, appears on The Voice US, plays spray tan bingo, meets his puppet self and celebrates a number one. Part two goes from Glastonbury to Japan, where Ed plays Thinking Out Loud at a cat cafe. Part three and a birthday special round off the series.
